TCGA Risk Pipeline

Multimodal cancer risk prediction across 6,242 patients and 31 cancer types. Histopathology slide features, pathology report text and clinical records are cleaned and joined on a medallion architecture in Databricks, then fused into one risk model. Every number on this page is read from the pipeline's own exported tables.

Model performance

Balanced accuracy on the held-out test split, which is the metric that matters here because high-risk patients are the minority class (585 of 6,242). One model trained across all cancer types beats every single-cancer-type model, so the shared signal across tumours outweighs the smaller per-type sample.

Risk labels by cancer type

Patients are labelled from overall-survival percentiles within their own cancer type: the shortest third survive to the 33rd percentile (high risk), the longest third past the 67th (low risk), and everyone in between is ambiguous. Censored patients who have not reached the threshold stay ambiguous rather than being guessed at, which is why that band dominates. Twelve largest cohorts shown.

Where the cohort comes from

The three sources cover different patients. Joining on patients present in all three is what sets the usable cohort size, and it is the single biggest constraint in the pipeline.
